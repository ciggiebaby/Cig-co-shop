## 2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to take an order, fulfill it, support you, keep records, and (where allowed) contact you.

### Cig & Co. orders (website and marketplaces)

- **Order and shipping information**, such as your name, email address, shipping address, phone number if you give us one, and what you ordered.

That includes the Arcade email gate (your email identifies your plays, and prize codes are sent to that address), the optional promotional checkbox on the Arcade and at checkout, back-in-stock "notify me" requests, and other list signups on the site.

### CIG Ventures Advisory (reports and subscriptions)

- **Intake answers and business information** used to fulfill the report or subscription you ordered. Today that includes: business name, website, industry, target geography, the type of company you want to reach, competitors to watch (public business names and websites only), the product ordered, a delivery email, an optional needed-by date, and short product-specific brief answers.
- We do **not** ask for passwords, banking or payout details, Social Security or tax-ID numbers, card numbers, or personal data about your customers or employees. If someone sends that kind of information by mistake, we do not keep those fields and we ask for a clean copy.

### Business contact information for B2B email

For lawful B2B outreach about Advisory services, we may use **business contact information from public sources** only — for example, a general business email, contact form, or main business line published by the company. We do not collect personal profile data from LinkedIn, Facebook, Google Maps, or Yelp, and we do not use personal or employee home contact details.

We do not collect payment card numbers through our intake form or by email.

## 3. Website analytics, cookies, and similar technologies

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics pixels on cigcompany.com. A scan of the live site and the site source found no Meta (Facebook) Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Analytics, gtag, Vercel Analytics, or Speed Insights.

The homepage includes a Pinterest p:domain_verify meta tag. That tag is only for domain verification. It is not a Pinterest tracking pixel, and we do not load Pinterest's pintrk script.

We do use a **first-party visit log** on cigcompany.com to understand how the shop is used (for example, which pages are viewed, which products are added to the bag, and where checkout starts or stops). On each event, our site sends a small record to our own hosting function, which stores it in our database. That record can include the page path, the event type, a product id, the referring site, campaign tags from the URL (src and utm tags), whether the device appears to be a phone or a desktop, a random per-tab session id, and the visitor's country as provided by our host. It does not use cookies. It does not store an IP address, a user-agent string, an email address, or a shipping address.

The per-tab session id and campaign tags are kept in the browser's session storage (not a cookie) so we can count visits in the same tab and keep a landing campaign with later events in that tab. We also keep landing src and utm tags in session storage and pass them with an order to Stripe or PayPal as checkout metadata, so we can see which link led to a sale. A checkout "fingerprint" is a hash of the cart contents used to keep the payment session consistent; it is not a device identifier and is not used for advertising.

If your browser sends a **Do Not Track (DNT)** or **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal, we do not send the first-party visit record. If we later add advertising tools, we will honor GPC for those tools as the law requires.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when your browser requests those fonts. Stripe and PayPal may also set their own cookies on their hosted checkout pages, which are covered by their privacy policies.

## 5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We share information only as needed to run the business:

- **Payment processors:** Stripe and PayPal, to process payments. Their privacy policies apply to the data they collect on their pages.
- **Fulfillment:** Printful, to produce and ship Cig & Co. products.
- **Email and communications:** Zoho Mail, to operate our inboxes (including info@cigcompany.com and ventures@cigcompany.com); Resend, to send transactional and list email (such as order, shipment, and Arcade prize messages); and Kit, to send promotional email to people who have opted in.
- **Website host and database:** Netlify hosts cigcompany.com and related functions. Supabase stores order, sign-up, and first-party visit records.
- **Marketplaces:** Etsy and TikTok Shop operate under their own privacy policies when you buy or sell there. We do not control those services.
- **Professional helpers** who need the information to do work for us (for example, bookkeeping or, if retained, legal counsel), under a duty to keep it confidential.
- **Legal and safety:** if we are required by law, or to protect our rights, customers, or the public.

We do not share personal information with third parties so they can market their own products.

## 6. Marketing emails, texts, calls, and opt-out

### Email

We may send:

- **Cig & Co.** product, drop, or promo emails to people who opt in, or as otherwise allowed by law.
- **CIG Ventures Advisory** emails to a business contact, including cold B2B email that uses public business contact information only.

Every marketing or cold outreach email will identify us, include our postal address, and provide a working way to opt out, as required by the CAN-SPAM Act. You can opt out by using the unsubscribe link in the email or by writing to info@cigcompany.com (Advisory recipients may also email ventures@cigcompany.com). We will honor opt-out requests. We may still send non-marketing messages about an order you placed.

**Cig & Co. customer data is not used to market other businesses, including CIG Ventures Advisory, without the customer's consent.** A Cig & Co. purchase or a Cig & Co. mailing-list signup is not consent to receive Advisory marketing. Any introduction to Advisory is a separate, consented, or otherwise lawful contact, with an opt-out, and is not a reason to add that person to a recurring Advisory list.

### Marketing texts and calls

We may send marketing texts or make marketing calls only with your prior express written consent. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for help. We do not sell or share phone numbers or consent with third parties for their marketing.

## 9. Your rights

You may ask us to **access**, **correct**, or **delete** personal information we hold about you, subject to records we must keep for legal, payment, or bookkeeping reasons. To make a request, email info@cigcompany.com (Advisory clients may also email ventures@cigcompany.com). We may need to verify the request.

### California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to:

- Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
- Request deletion or correction of personal information.
- Opt out of the "sale" of personal information, or of "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, if we did either of those things.
- Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise these rights, email info@cigcompany.com. You may also use an authorized agent, as California law allows. We will not charge a fee for a request, or deny goods or services, because you exercised these rights.

### Do-Not-Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting. There is no common industry standard for responding to DNT signals. For the first-party visit log described in section 3, we honor both DNT and Global Privacy Control (GPC): if either signal is present, we do not record the visit. If we later add advertising tools, we will honor GPC for those tools as the law requires.

### Visitors in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom

If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom, we process your information under the GDPR or UK GDPR on these legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract**, when we take, pay for, fulfill, or deliver an order or an Advisory report or subscription.
- **Legitimate interests**, such as running and securing the site, keeping first-party visit records, preventing fraud, and making lawful B2B contact using public business information, in a way we believe is proportionate and does not override your rights.
- **Consent**, for optional marketing emails, optional mailing-list or Arcade or checkout signups, and marketing texts or calls. You may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation**, such as tax, accounting, and other records the law requires us to keep.

You may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your data, to object to certain uses (including legitimate-interest processing and direct marketing), to request a copy of data you provided to us (data portability), and to withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Because we are based in the United States, your information is transferred to and processed in the United States. Our service providers also process data in the United States. Where those providers offer a lawful transfer tool, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, we rely on that tool. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (for example, the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom).
